Questions people ask before they switch

Straight answers on what QMS Guardian is, who it fits, and what it costs.

What is QMS Guardian?

A hosted ISO 9001 quality management system for small manufacturers. It keeps nonconformance and CAPA, calibration tracking, internal audits, training records and document control in one place, instead of spread across spreadsheets and a binder.

Who is it for?

Manufacturing shops of roughly 100 people or fewer that need a real QMS for ISO 9001 or IATF 16949 but don't have a dedicated quality department or an enterprise software budget.

Does it cover ISO 9001?

Yes. The modules map to the ISO 9001:2015 clauses — nonconformity and corrective action (8.7, 10.2), calibration and monitoring resources (7.1.5), internal audit (9.2), competence and training (7.2), and documented information (7.5) — and a clause readiness view shows where you stand.

How long does it take to set up?

A shop can stand up its own workflows, roles and record types in an afternoon. Every plan includes every module, so there's nothing to add on later.

Is there a free trial?

No self-serve free trial. A live demo on your own roles and data is available on request — email shawnparnell81@yahoo.com.

What does it cost?

Three flat monthly plans, billed annually: Starter $399/month, Growth $799/month, and Business $1,299/month. One price per plan, no per-module add-ons.
