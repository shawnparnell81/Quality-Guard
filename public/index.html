Why a gap appears

A gap is not a stored flag. The server computes it at read time from two tables: which documents a role is required to be trained on, and which training records exist at the document's current revision. Release a new revision in Document Control and the gaps appear on their own. This is the link auditors probe hardest under clause 7.2.

People and Access5.3

Who works here, what they are authorised to do, and who granted it. Access is withdrawn, never deleted, so the history stays readable.

Directory

NameJob titleDiscipline RolePermissionsAccessActions
How authority is assigned

A person holds one role, and the role carries the permissions. Nobody is granted an authority directly, which means the answer to "who can approve a use-as-is disposition" is always a short list of roles rather than an audit of every individual account.

Discipline is recorded separately because it describes what somebody works on, not what they may do. A mechanical and an electrical engineer hold identical authority; splitting them into two roles would double this matrix without adding a single rule.

Add a person: user.create Change a role: user.edit Withdraw access: user.deactivate Change what a role may do: roles.manage
